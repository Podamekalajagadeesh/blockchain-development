
# Blockchain Concepts

1. Blockchain: A chain of connected blocks.
2. Block: A container for data.
3. Transaction: An exchange of value or information.
4. Hash: A fixed-length digital fingerprint.
5. SHA-256: A cryptographic hashing algorithm.
6. Genesis Block: The first block.
7. Previous Hash: The hash of the preceding block.
8. Node: A computer participating in a blockchain network.
9. Consensus: A process for agreeing on blockchain state.
10. Immutability: Resistance to changing recorded data.
